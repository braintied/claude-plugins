# Reference shelf

Live sites to offer in stage 1 when the person has no references, sorted by the world
layer they show best. Each line says what the first screen does, as seen on a 1440px
screenshot in October 2026. Sites change: open the site before offering it, and offer
the move, not the brand.

These are references for a *move*, never for a layout to copy. In `references.json`
write what each one governs (stage 2), and pick ones whose first screen shows that move,
because stage 6 compares first screens.

## Photographic: the photograph is the page

| Site | What the first screen does |
|---|---|
| [Ottolenghi](https://www.ottolenghi.co.uk) | A full-bleed kitchen photograph or film (knife on a board, fire under a grill) with a short, plain headline set large over it ("Sunday lunch at ROVI"). Food without a plated flat-lay. |
| [Rapha](https://www.rapha.cc) | One macro photograph fills the screen (a wet shoe, beads of rain); a condensed uppercase headline sits on it, left and low. Texture does the selling. |
| [Graza](https://www.graza.co) | A candid kitchen photograph, hands mid-task, full bleed; a condensed serif headline over the lower left; one bright button. Playful words, serious photography. |
| [Omsom](https://omsom.com) | A styled food photograph, saturated and flash-lit, with a hand-made graphic sticker on top. A photograph plus one graphic layer. |
| [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com) | A dim, warm full-bleed photograph (a hand reaching to a shelf) with small, quiet type centred low. Restraint: the type is small because the picture is the voice. |
| [Teenage Engineering](https://teenage.engineering) | A black-and-white photograph of a shop front at night, a neon sign as the headline. Navigation drawn as a technical diagram along the top. |
| [MUBI](https://mubi.com) | A film still, full bleed, with one huge uppercase line and the signup form directly on the image. |

## Type-led and editorial: the type is the image

| Site | What the first screen does |
|---|---|
| [Monocle](https://monocle.com) | A masthead at poster size in a classic serif, a ruled newspaper grid under it, small caps section labels. A printed object, set online. |
| [Kinfolk](https://www.kinfolk.com) | One magazine cover, centred, on a white page with a thin serif headline. The product shown as an object, nothing else. |
| [Cereal](https://readcereal.com) | A catalogue index: thin rules, small type, a grid of small photographs with dates. Calm density. |
| [Oatly](https://www.oatly.com) | A hand-made, slightly crude layout: uppercase type in boxes, photographs pasted in, a deliberate off-grid feel. The voice is the design. |
| [Rauno Freiberg](https://rauno.me) | One very large sentence about the person and a single large yellow circle behind it. One graphic gesture. |

## Studio showcases: what the top end of motion looks like

These open with preloaders and WebGL scenes, so screenshots often catch a loading
screen. Visit them to feel the ceiling, not to copy: [Lusion](https://lusion.co),
